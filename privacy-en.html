Word Hop · Privacy Policy

Effective / last updated: 2026-11-04

Applies to the iOS app “单词快跑” (Word Hop, bundle ID com.evens.wordhop), version 0.3.0 and later. If this translation differs from the Simplified Chinese version, the Chinese version prevails.

In short: the app itself does not collect any of your data. The app needs no account and has no ads, analytics or tracking. The game makes no network requests and sends nothing to the developer or any third party. Your learning progress and settings stay on your device, and you can clear them in the app at any time.

We receive information about you in only two cases: when you choose to email us (see section 7), and when you choose in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, in which case Apple may give us aggregated data and crash reports that do not identify you (see section 6).

1. Who is responsible for the app

The app is developed and distributed by Tongji Wu (“we”). See section 9 for how to contact us.

2. Information stored on your device

To keep track of your practice, the app saves the following in its local app storage. It is processed only on your device and is never uploaded to us or to third parties:

  • Learning progress: experience points and level, journeys completed, questions answered and first-try correct answers, coins, gatekeepers defeated, best streak and best scores, places visited, achievements, right/wrong counts for each word, and the results of your 12 most recent journeys.
  • Settings: the selected word bank, mode, difficulty, layout, speed and character, and whether sound and speech are on.

The app does not access your contacts, photos, location, microphone, camera or other apps’ data, and does not use the advertising identifier.

3. How to clear your progress

  1. On the home screen, tap “… · 个人档案” (Profile; the first part shows your current title).
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap “清除全部记录” (Clear all records).
  3. When the button changes to “再点一次，确认清除全部记录” (Tap again to confirm), tap it again. The message “本机学习档案已清除” confirms the progress was cleared.

Clearing resets your learning progress and cannot be undone; your settings (such as word bank and sound) are kept. Deleting the app removes all data it stored on the device.

4. Device backups

Like other apps’ data, this app’s local data may be included in iCloud Backup or computer backups, depending on your system settings. Backups are managed by Apple and your device settings; we cannot access them. You can manage backups under iCloud Backup in iOS Settings or as described by Apple.

5. System speech

When speech is on, the app passes the current word to the speech synthesizer built into iOS. Speech is handled by iOS; we do not receive what is spoken or any voice data. Apple’s handling of system features is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy. Available languages and voices depend on the system voices installed on your device; if no voice for a language is available, the app does not speak. You can turn sound or speech off in the app.

6. Information provided by Apple

When you download the app from the App Store, Apple processes the download and transaction. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may give us aggregated usage data and crash reports that do not identify you, which we may use to improve the app. This information is collected and aggregated by Apple’s system features and then provided to us; it is not collected or sent by the app, which contains no analytics or crash-reporting code. You control this sharing in Settings and can turn off “Share With App Developers” at any time under Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements (menu names may vary between iOS versions).

7. When you contact us

If you email us (for example, to report a problem or ask about this policy), we receive your email address, the content of your message and anything else you choose to include, such as your device model, iOS version, app version, chosen language and word bank, and steps to reproduce a problem. You send this information yourself; the app does not collect it automatically. Please don’t include personal information that isn’t needed to resolve your issue.

  • Use: only to reply to you and to investigate and fix the problem you reported.
  • Sharing: we do not sell this information or share it with third parties. Emails are sent, received and stored through the email service we use (Gmail), which handles them under its own privacy policy.
  • Retention: we delete these emails within 12 months after the issue you reported is resolved.
  • Deletion: you can ask us at any time, by emailing annamelessguy@gmail.com, to delete your correspondence with us and the information in it, and we will delete it once we receive your request.

8. Children

Word Hop is a vocabulary practice game and may be used by children and other minors. On the App Store it is rated 4+ and listed in the Education category; it is not part of the App Store’s Kids category. The app itself does not collect personal information from anyone, including children:

  • No account is needed, and the app never asks for a name, age, email address or similar details.
  • There are no ads, no analytics or tracking, the advertising identifier is not used, and the game does not go online.
  • The app contains no third-party SDKs, no in-app purchases and no links that lead outside the app.
  • Learning progress stays on the device and is never uploaded to us (see section 2).

The only way a child could give us personal information is by emailing us. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Children under 14 (or under a higher age set by the law where they live) should ask a parent or guardian for permission before emailing us.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us personal information by email, please contact us as described in section 9 and we will delete the emails and the information in them.

9. Contact

For questions about this policy or your data, contact: annamelessguy@gmail.com.

10. Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles data changes (for example, by adding cloud sync, analytics or other online features), we will update this policy and the date at the top first, and mention significant changes in the app’s release notes. The app will not start collecting data without this policy being updated first.
