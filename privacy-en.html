Word Hop · Privacy Policy

Effective / last updated: 2026-10-19

Applies to the iOS app “单词快跑” (Word Hop, bundle ID com.evens.wordhop), version 0.3.0 and later. If this translation differs from the Simplified Chinese version, the Chinese version prevails.

In short: we do not collect any of your data. The app needs no account and has no ads, analytics or tracking. The game makes no network requests and sends nothing to the developer or any third party. Your learning progress and settings stay on your device, and you can clear them in the app at any time.

1. Who is responsible for the app

The app is developed and distributed by Tongji Wu (“we”). See section 8 for how to contact us.

2. Information stored on your device

To keep track of your practice, the app saves the following in its local app storage. It is processed only on your device and is never uploaded to us or to third parties:

  • Learning progress: experience points and level, journeys completed, questions answered and first-try correct answers, coins, gatekeepers defeated, best streak and best scores, places visited, achievements, right/wrong counts for each word, and the results of your 12 most recent journeys.
  • Settings: the selected word bank, mode, difficulty, layout, speed and character, and whether sound and speech are on.

The app does not access your contacts, photos, location, microphone, camera or other apps’ data, and does not use the advertising identifier.

3. How to clear your progress

  1. On the home screen, tap “… · 个人档案” (Profile; the first part shows your current title).
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap “清除全部记录” (Clear all records).
  3. When the button changes to “再点一次，确认清除全部记录” (Tap again to confirm), tap it again. The message “本机学习档案已清除” confirms the progress was cleared.

Clearing resets your learning progress and cannot be undone; your settings (such as word bank and sound) are kept. Deleting the app removes all data it stored on the device.

4. Device backups

Like other apps’ data, this app’s local data may be included in iCloud Backup or computer backups, depending on your system settings. Backups are managed by Apple and your device settings; we cannot access them. You can manage backups under iCloud Backup in iOS Settings or as described by Apple.

5. System speech

When speech is on, the app passes the current word to the speech synthesizer built into iOS. Speech is handled by iOS; we do not receive what is spoken or any voice data. Apple’s handling of system features is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy. Available languages and voices depend on the system voices installed on your device; if no voice for a language is available, the app does not speak. You can turn sound or speech off in the app.

6. Information provided by Apple

When you download the app from the App Store, Apple processes the download and transaction. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may give us aggregated usage data and crash reports that do not identify you, which we may use to improve the app. This sharing is handled by Apple and controlled by you in Settings; the app itself contains no analytics or crash-reporting code.

7. Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy or your data, contact: annamelessguy@gmail.com.

9. Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles data changes (for example, by adding cloud sync, analytics or other online features), we will update this policy and the date at the top first, and mention significant changes in the app’s release notes. We will not start collecting data without first updating this policy.
