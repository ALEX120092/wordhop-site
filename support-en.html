How to play

Word Hop is a vocabulary practice game for English, German, Spanish and French.

  • Press and drag on the road; your character follows your finger’s relative movement.
  • Walk through the door with the correct answer; you can steer around words you don’t know.
  • Tap the hourglass to slow down, and the top-right button to pause.
  • At the end of a journey your results are tallied and saved to your on-device profile.

The A1–C2 levels are modelled on the CEFR as a study guide only. They are not an official CEFR certification, a proficiency test or a promise of exam results.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No account is needed. All practice content ships with the app, and the game itself does not go online.

There is no sound, or words are not spoken

  • Make sure sound and speech are switched on in the app, and turn up the media volume.
  • The app follows your iPhone’s silent mode; no sound plays while the silent switch or silent mode is on.
  • Speech uses your device’s system voices. If a language is not spoken, download a voice for it in iOS Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > Voices (menu names may vary between iOS versions). Without a matching voice the app does not speak.

Where is my progress stored? Can I continue on another device?

Your progress is stored only on this device and is never uploaded. The iOS profile is separate from the web, WeChat and desktop editions, and there is no cross-device sync. Deleting the app deletes the on-device profile.

Why am I back on the home screen after returning to the app?

The game pauses automatically when the app goes to the background. If iOS closed the app while it was in the background, it reopens on the home screen: finished journeys are kept, but an unfinished journey is not restored.

How do I clear my progress?

On the home screen tap “… · 个人档案” (Profile), scroll to the bottom, tap “清除全部记录” (Clear all records), then tap again to confirm. This cannot be undone; your settings are kept. See section 3 of the privacy policy.

Does the app collect my data?

No. See the privacy policy.
